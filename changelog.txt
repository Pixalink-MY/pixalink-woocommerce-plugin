*** Pixalink Loyalty for WooCommerce Changelog ***

= 0.1.0 - unreleased =
* Add - Plugin scaffold: requirements checks (PHP 8.1, sodium, WooCommerce 11.0+), HPOS and Cart & Checkout blocks compatibility declarations.
* Add - Install lifecycle: activation seeds options, deactivation unschedules work, opt-in data removal on uninstall.
* Add - Support layer: encrypted credentials (sodium secretbox), masked WooCommerce logging, RFC 4122 UUIDs, money helpers, server-aligned clock.
* Add - Signed Pixalink Commerce API client with timestamp-skew recovery and the Connect resource.
* Add - WooCommerce › Settings › Pixalink Loyalty: connect an existing Pixalink account (hosted sign-in with PKCE) or paste an outlet API key, request access, test and disconnect.
* Add - Six-hourly connection heartbeat with remote feature switches, clock alignment and a "reconnect" state when Pixalink rejects the key.
* Add - Detects old Pixalink WooCommerce webhooks and offers to pause them when connecting.
* Add - Admin notices for a missing connection, a rejected key, an outdated plugin, an inactive Pixalink account and disabled coupons.
* Add - Loyalty phone number (stored in international format) and a separate, unticked marketing-consent box at checkout (Checkout block and classic), registration and Account details; the wording shown is recorded as a hash with every answer.
* Add - My Account › Loyalty: phone verification with a one-time code (WhatsApp or SMS), points, credits, tier, vouchers, memberships, recent activity and a member QR code; `[pixalink_loyalty_summary]` and `[pixalink_loyalty_qr]` shortcodes.
* Add - Sign-in from the Pixalink member portal (`pixalink_sso` links), customer updates to Pixalink when a shopper changes their phone or consent, and a signed webhook from Pixalink that refreshes balances and records withdrawn consent.
* Add - Personal data export and erasure for the loyalty account data, and suggested privacy policy text.
* Add - Vouchers, points and credits at checkout as virtual WooCommerce coupons, priced by a Pixalink quote, in the Checkout block (order summary panel, relabelled coupon chips) and the classic checkout (loyalty panel); only phone-verified members can use them.
* Add - Points, credits and vouchers are held when the order is placed and released when payment fails, the order is cancelled or it is abandoned (15-minute sweep).
* Add - Order-received page: points earned or when they will be added, and the rewards the order used; order screen: held and settled redemptions.
* Add - WooCommerce › Settings › Pixalink Loyalty › Checkout: credits toggle; each toggle shows when Pixalink switched the feature off.
* Add - My Account dashboard: a loyalty card with points, the next expiry, tier progress and vouchers ready, or a link to verify on the Loyalty tab; "Dashboard summary" setting under My Account (on by default).
* Fix - A Pixalink portal link opened while signed in as staff (editor, shop manager, administrator) no longer links that account to the member: nothing is linked or signed in, a notice says why, and `pixalink_loyalty_sso_refused_staff` fires. Staff accounts cannot be linked by a one-time code or a guest link moved at login either.
* Fix - A member the portal recognises but who stays a guest (store registration off, or no e-mail address) now sees a notice that they will earn points on this visit, with how to create an account; the Loyalty tab login form and `[pixalink_loyalty_summary]` say the same.
* Add - "Match your member portal" under WooCommerce › Settings › Pixalink Loyalty › My Account (on by default): My Account and both checkouts use the member portal's logo and colours from the heartbeat's `branding`, as CSS custom properties (`--pxl-primary`, `--pxl-on-primary`, `--pxl-ink`, `--pxl-accent`, `--pxl-tier`) scoped to the plugin's own wrappers; off, the same layout takes the theme's colours.
* Add - My Account › Loyalty redesigned: a member card (portal logo or name, tier with its colour, points, credits, the next points to expire, tier progress, member name and the member code behind "Show member code"), vouchers as tickets marked "Use at checkout" or "In store only", memberships, recent activity with signed point changes, and empty states; the dashboard card is a compact member card; a tidier phone verification form.
* Add - Portal buttons open the Pixalink member portal signed in for verified members: "View all rewards", "Full points history", "Credits" (while credits are on) and "Open member portal" go through `admin-post.php?action=pixalink_loyalty_open_portal`, which asks Pixalink for a single-use link at the click (`POST customers/{id}/portal-session`) and redirects with no-cache headers and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; anything unexpected falls back to the signed-out portal. New action `pixalink_loyalty_portal_handoff` (user id, destination, outcome).
* Tweak - Checkout loyalty panel (Checkout block and classic): portal colours on its buttons, points slider and applied voucher, and compact buttons.
* Tweak - Template versions: `myaccount/loyalty.php`, `myaccount/dashboard-card.php`, `myaccount/link-account.php` and `checkout/loyalty-panel.php` are now 0.3.0; themes that override them should update their copies.
* Add - "Sign in with {portal name}" under the My Account and classic checkout login forms (not the Checkout block, which has no login form), in the portal's logo and colours while "Match your member portal" is on. It goes through `admin-post.php?action=pixalink_loyalty_portal_sign_in`, which keeps a random state in the `pixalink_loyalty_sso_state` cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on https, 10 minutes) and sends the browser (303, no cache, no Referer) to the heartbeat's `branding.sign_in_url` with `return` and `state`; the portal comes back with a `pixalink_sso` ticket, redeemed with `sso_state` from the cookie, which is then cleared. Shown only to logged-out shoppers while the store is connected and the heartbeat names a sign-in page on the portal's host.
* Add - "Sign in with your member portal" under WooCommerce › Settings › Pixalink Loyalty › My Account (`pixalink_loyalty_portal_sign_in`, on by default). A member who signs in with the button gets a customer account even with store registration off, as long as Pixalink has their e-mail address; without one they stay a recognised guest. The portal's Order links are unchanged. `pixalink_loyalty_sso_create_account` gets a third argument, whether the sign-in started from the button.
* Add - New template `global/portal-sign-in.php` (0.3.0) and stylesheet `assets/css/sign-in.css`; the button reads `--pxl-primary`, `--pxl-on-primary` and `--pxl-ink` on `.pixalink-loyalty-sign-in`.
* Fix - A portal button clicked after the store session ended no longer gets WordPress's blank 400: it goes to the My Account login form with `redirect_to` back to the Loyalty tab, without any call to Pixalink.
* Dev - CI (PHPCS, PHPStan level 8, JS build, PHPUnit unit/contract/integration with HPOS on and off, Plugin Check, Playwright checkout e2e) and release pipeline.
